# Abstract

In the vehicle insurance industry, accurately evaluating risk and determining fair premium pricing is paramount. Traditional actuarial approaches, particularly Generalized Linear Models (GLMs), heavily rely on rigid boundary structures that inherently struggle to capture the complex, non-linear intersections between human behavior and risk characteristics. This limitation systematically results in high prediction errors, suboptimal risk segmentation, and an increased risk of financial exposure for insurers. 

To simultaneously address these fundamental challenges, this research developed and deployed a novel hybrid predictive engine that strategically integrates an Adaptive Neuro-Fuzzy Inference System (ANFIS) with a Deep Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) meta-learner. Initially, the ANFIS mathematically translates rigid, numerical policyholder data into continuous, fluid risk categories using actively optimized Gaussian membership functions. A deep MLP Stacker then seamlessly orchestrates the architecture; it successfully analyzes out-of-fold predictions from both traditional GLM baselines and the ANFIS model to dynamically formulate a functionally optimized pure premium quote. Finally, an explicit Isotonic Regression calibration layer ensures undeniable systemic, real-world solvency.

This proposed framework significantly enhances the current state of the art by successfully bridging the divide between classical actuarial interpretability and the intricate predictive capabilities of modern deep machine learning. Rather than operating as an unexplainable "black-box," the model actively isolates the most severe drivers while cleanly overlapping standard risk segments. This explicit eradication of static mathematical boundaries uniquely drives a massive reduction in the overall Mean Absolute Error (improving from 353.90 down to 228.85) alongside aggressive improvements in risk-ranking quality (raising the Gini Index from 0.1556 to 0.1689). Crucially, the engineered solution entirely overcomes the dangerous systemic under-pricing tendencies of pure machine learning engines by confidently guaranteeing a structurally safe 1.015 Predicted-to-Observed ratio. Ultimately, this rigorously proven framework safely empowers modern underwriting teams with a highly superior, deeply transparent, and entirely financially solvent operational alternative to legacy pricing methodologies.
